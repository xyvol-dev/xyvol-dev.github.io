Privacy Policy

Rings Away: Twist Puzzle

Last updated: November 10, 2026

In brief: Rings Away: Twist Puzzle is designed as a self-contained puzzle game. The current version does not require a player account and does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Your puzzle progress and settings are ordinarily kept on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Rings Away: Twist Puzzle ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Rings Away: Twist Puzzle mobile application (the "App"). It applies to information handled through the App and when you contact us about it.

1. Information the App uses

The core puzzle experience is intended to work without asking you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health information.

The App saves game-related information locally on your device so that it can remember your preferences and continue your play. This may include completed stages, current puzzle state, sound or haptic settings, and similar local game settings. This local information is not transmitted to us by the App as part of normal gameplay.

2. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, device details, screenshots, and the description of your question. We use that information only to respond, troubleshoot, protect the App, and maintain a record of the support interaction. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

3. Apple and device services

Your device and Apple may process information when you download, purchase, update, or use the App. For example, Apple may handle App Store transactions, receipts, device-level diagnostics, or iCloud backup according to your device settings and Apple's own policies. We do not receive your Apple ID, payment-card number, or complete App Store payment information from Apple.

If a future version offers an optional Apple service, such as Game Center, the applicable Apple account and privacy controls will apply to that service. We will update this policy before adding a feature that changes the App's data practices.

4. No advertising tracking in the current App

The current App is not designed to use third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or an advertising identifier to follow you across apps or websites. We do not use the local gameplay information described above to build advertising profiles.

5. How support information is used and shared

We use support information to answer your message, diagnose and resolve a reported issue, maintain the security and operation of the App, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or disclose it for targeted advertising.

We may disclose information only when reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce or protect our rights, investigate misuse, protect the safety of users or others, or respond to a valid legal process. If our business is involved in a merger, acquisition, or transfer, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

6. Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it, delete the App, or reset it through the device or App controls. Depending on your Apple backup settings, a backup may retain App data until it is removed under Apple's processes. Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited information we handle. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device and keeping its operating system current.

8. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support without appropriate authorization, contact us and we will review the request and take appropriate action.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

You can delete local game data by deleting the App or using available reset controls. You may also manage App permissions and iCloud backup through your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email us at the address below. We may need enough information to verify and process the request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the App or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be made available with the App and will show a new "Last updated" date. If a change materially affects how personal information is handled, we will provide additional notice when required.
